# 🌍 DX Clock — Your HF World at a Glance

**Turn on your radio. What's happening on the bands?**

Which DXpeditions are currently active? Are the bands open? Where is the activity? What can you hear right now?

**DX Clock** brings all that information together in one clean, real-time dashboard, inspired by the classic HamClock concept but designed for the modern web.

No installation. No accounts. No complicated setup.

### 📡 [Launch DX Clock](https://zp5dxs.github.io/dxclock/)

## What can you see?

### 🌐 Live World Activity Map
Explore reported amateur radio activity around the world.

- CW, SSB and FT8 activity.
- Select individual HF bands or view them all.
- Color-coded bands and different marker shapes for each mode.
- Interactive map with zoom and navigation.
- Highlighted DXpeditions with their callsigns.

### 🏝️ DXpeditions — Never Miss the Action
DXpeditions deserve special attention.

DX Clock automatically follows announced expeditions and matches their callsigns against recent DX spots.

- Active and upcoming DXpeditions.
- Most frequently spotted expeditions prioritized.
- Operating modes and reported bands or frequencies.
- Spot counts and last reported activity.
- Country flags and geographic locations.

Even when an expedition isn't being spotted at that moment, it remains visible during its announced operating period.

### 📊 HF Band Conditions
Quickly check the state of the HF bands before choosing where to operate.

- Relative activity levels based on observed spots.
- Daytime and nighttime propagation estimates.
- Easy-to-read visual indicators.
- Solar Flux Index (SFI), A-index and K-index.

### 📻 Live DX Cluster
See which stations are being reported on the air.

- CW, SSB and FT8 filters, including a combined view.
- Callsigns, country flags, bands and frequencies.
- Repeated reports grouped together for a cleaner display.
- Automatic updates.

### 🕒 Your Shack Clock
A large UTC clock keeps universal time front and center.

- Secondary clock with configurable UTC offset.
- Customizable station callsign.
- Light, dark and deep-black themes.
- Full-screen mode.
- Preferences saved locally in your browser.

## 📺 Built for Your Shack

DX Clock is designed to work directly in a modern web browser on desktop computers, laptops, tablets, smartphones and compatible Smart TVs.

Open it on a spare monitor, put it in full-screen mode, and turn it into a live information display for your amateur radio station.

**One screen. Your bands. Your DX. Your world.**

## ⚙️ How It Works

DX Clock is a lightweight web application hosted on GitHub Pages, using HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

It combines information from:

- **HamQTH** — DX Cluster reports.
- **Vail ReRBN** — CW and digital-mode reception reports.
- **NG3K ADXO** — DXpedition calendar information.
- **HF propagation data** — solar indicators and general band-condition estimates.

Data availability depends on the external sources.

**Important:** DX spots and reception reports are observations, not confirmed QSOs. Band activity and general propagation estimates do not guarantee that a station will be audible from your location.
